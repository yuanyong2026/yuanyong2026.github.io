📝 煎服法 / Preparation & Dosage

  1. 汤剂煎服 / Decoction: 每日一剂，水煎两次，合并药汁，分早晚两次温服。
    One packet daily. Simmer twice, combine the liquid, take warm morning and evening.
  2. 粉剂冲服 / Herbal Powder: 每次一匙（约3-5g），温水冲服，每日两次，早晚各一次。
    One spoon (approx. 3-5g) mixed in warm water, twice daily (morning and evening).
  3. 疗程 / Duration: 连服 ___ 天后复诊，根据舌象和症状变化调方。
    Take for ___ days, then follow up for formula adjustment based on tongue and symptoms.

⚠️ 注意事项 / Precautions

  • 服药期间忌生冷、辛辣、油腻之物。
    Avoid raw, cold, spicy, and greasy food during treatment.
  • 孕妇、哺乳期女性请告知医师。
    Inform the practitioner if pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • 如出现不适，暂停服药并联系医师。
    If discomfort occurs, stop and contact the practitioner.
  • 本处方为个人辨证用药，请勿与他人共用。
    This prescription is personalized — do not share with others.
